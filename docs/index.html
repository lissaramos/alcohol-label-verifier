Getting Started

Alcohol Label Verifier checks label photos against what was submitted on a TTB application — brand name, class/type, alcohol content, net contents, government warning, name/address, and sulfite declaration where it applies — flagging mismatches and close calls for a quick human review.

How to verify a label

  1. Go to Verify Labels and choose a beverage type (distilled spirits, wine, or beer/malt beverage).
  2. Enter the fields exactly as submitted on the application, and upload one photo per label surface (front, back, neck, strip) — don't combine multiple surfaces into one image.
  3. Click Add to queue — repeat for as many labels as needed, or use Bulk import from CSV to add many at once from a spreadsheet.
  4. Click Verify all to run the checks.
  5. Open Results to see each label's outcome — Pass, Fail, or Needs Review — and open any entry for a full field-by-field breakdown, including the option to manually mark a flagged field as a match or mismatch.

What gets checked

  • Brand name / class-type — fuzzy-matched, so near-identical text (different capitalization, minor punctuation) still passes automatically.
  • Alcohol content — category-specific rules (tight tolerance for spirits, legal tolerance bands for wine, optional for beer).
  • Net contents — volume and unit compared, tolerant of unit formatting.
  • Government warning — exact match against the legally required text; no tolerance.
  • Name and address of the bottler/producer — required on every label.
  • Sulfite declaration — checked on wine and malt beverages.

Verify Labels

Enter what was submitted on the application, and upload a photo for each label surface (front, back, neck, strip) — one per photo, not combined into one image. The government warning is checked automatically — no need to enter it.
